## A Long Time for a Little
It is hard to say exactly what needs said.

Hold a 2 hour meeting. The audience takeaway from those 120 minutes probably couldn't fill a standard index card.

An index card comfortably holds about ~60 words. That means about a 2 minute per word ratio.

# Top 5: Phone-less Observation

From my first day of leaving my phone next to the bathroom sink.
## 5. Not filling the cracks of time between things makes you notice there *is* time between things

"I just have no time" is a lie your phone makes you tell yourself.

## 4. This is [The Medium Method](https://gillespedia.com/Medium+Method) incarnate

The Medium Method suggests using different *mediums* to do your work. Throughout the day, use *physical* pen and paper. At the end of the day, transcribe that which needs to be into digital systems.

I wrote these observations on an index card, by the way.
## 3. Peripheral devices make more sense

The capabilities of my watch and HomePod are much more noticeable. The devices I feel bad for not using enough I'm using.
## 2. Work was easier, but not "magically" so

Nuff said.

## 1. I am in the moment

I don't have to actively "choose" to pay attention to my surroundings. I'm actually amongst them. 

Time with family is time spent *with* the family. Listening comes easy. 

This is good.
