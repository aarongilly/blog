## WE'RE MOVING

A job opportunity came up for my wife back in the Blue Valley region south of Kansas City, where we used to live. That's closer to where I work, too. So we've gone **all-in** on operation back to KC this summer. The past 6 weeks have been a whirlwind.

> [!info] The next couple months are going to be interesting

## Why Move?

Between changing job situations, some friends and family moves, there's been "move back to KC" reasons piling up over time - building up a "let's move" pressure in the back of my mind. Recently the dam broke.  
# The People...

## ...Around Us

We don't leave Lawrence gladly. We were excited to raise a family here. The neighborhood we are leaving is *wonderful*, and growing more wonderful with time. We met some great people and our kids have made some really good friends. Things were only getting better. 
## ...We Owe Everything To

Our move wasn't supposed to happen this fast. It has put us into "**GO MODE**" more than we've ever been as a family unit. And *exactly* when we've needed it, the people we love and who love us have gone to **battle** for us, not because we asked them to, but **because they knew we needed it**. They aren't lending a helping hand, they are lending a helping heart, body, and soul. It is amazing and overwhelming, and I can never thank them enough. Melissa and I are eternally grateful.
