# AI will Influence "Normal"

AI has a certain *style* of writing. 

Everyone who regularly works with AI is being exposed to this style of writing. 

In effect, it's like we will all have share the same friend we go to for help with technical things and proofing things. 

I have already consciously adapted my writing style **to mimic the effective style of AI**. 

I'm accentuating this effect now, *but not by much*.

# Top 5: Annoying Little Things AI Does
## 5. Write things like Buzzfeed headlines

Actual examples from a recent conversation:

- It’s a small wording change but surprisingly powerful.
- If you want, I can also show you **one extremely nice 8-line change to your `doPost` function**
- If you want, I can also show you **3 tiny structural tweaks** that would make this script feel _very professional as a reference implementation_
## 4. Pretends it sees specific problems all the time

This feels like it has to be a "just me" situation, but ChatGPT, Gemini, etc frequently say things like:
"Ah yes, it's the old 'your specific problem' problem." Which makes it *seem* like an expert, even if the solution it suggests is a hallucination. 
## 3. Ruin em dashes

I like using dashes in my writing. I essentially always have. I usually use regular hyphens, but that's just out of convenience. Now if I see writing with em dashes – like this – then I assume AI wrote it – even if it didn't.
## 2. Suck up constantly

Everything I say or idea I have is so impressive to all my AI friends. I could say "I think the sky is blue because someone threw blue paint in the air" and I'd get complimented on how insightful the idea was and how well-phrased I couched my hypothesis.
## 1.  Just get things blatantly wrong

Just click the "solve this specific problem" button in the interface. Oh that button doesn't exist? You're right! I'm sorry.
