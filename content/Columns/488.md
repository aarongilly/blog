# Quality. Not Quantity.

If there's been a **overarching theme** in Aaron's life and mind for the past many months, it's been this:

> [!quote] Quality. Not quantity.

To put it bluntly, quantity sucks.  Quality is where it's at. Quantity gets you more overhead, more expense, less time, more frustration, and generally returns long past diminished. 

# Form & Range of Motion > Weight & Reps

Find that video.

This video makes an argument I've never heard before, but one I really like. Don't push yourself to get as much weight for as many reps and sets as possible. Push yourself to get as much *out* of every rep and set you do. Weight is *one* lever you can throw to do that, but far from the only one. Form. Range of motion. Tempo. Mind-muscle connection. You should be *more* focused on perfecting your movements than slopping weight around.
