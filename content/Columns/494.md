# Top 5: Self-assessed Productivity Weaknesses

I think about, read about, and write about productivity a lot. Where do I suck at it?

## 5. Target confusion

When I have too much on my plate, I will spread myself too thin. I will make an inch of progress on 12 things rather than 1 foot of progress on one thing.

I often talk about the benefits of **focus**, because people are prone to give the advice they themselves need.

This may be my worst trait, because it shows up in different forms 3 times in this Top 5.

## 4. Setting squishy goals

Fear of failure means you fail to learn. 

## 3. Systems abandonment & adoption cycles

You might be surprised to hear[^1] the guy who's maintained a [life-in-a-spreadsheet project](https://datajournal.guide) for over 13 years is prone to re-building the "good" in search of "the great". 

That said, [[482|#482 - How I Organize My Life (aka AIM 3.1)]] has continued to work well for the past 6 months. Thanks [[Auto-Properties Plugin (V2)]].
## 2. Boom & bust cycles

I'm getting better at this, but was very prone to letting things pile up, then working it *all* down through a Herculean effort. The opposite of "slow and steady".

## 1. Not focusing on first thing's first

I am not sure I've ever once ***truly*** "eaten the frog", so they say. I've joked about "if you want me to solve world hunger, ask me to cure cancer." 

I get scared of the biggest elephant in the room, so I pick a fight with the next biggest.
