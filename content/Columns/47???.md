# Preamble - What's My Goal Here?

Every time you eat you make a decision that impacts your [3 Resources](https://gillespedia.com/3+Personal+Resources).

1. **Energy** - am I eating something nutritious, that will boost my mood & effectiveness in my day, while supporting my health?
2. **Time** - did this take me a long time to prepare, or go get?
3. **Money** - am I spending a lot of money on this?

And yet, we often default to some convenient/easy choice. For some folks, often times including myself, this "default choice" is a really *bad* one. It doesn't have to be.

> [!SUCCESS] Goal: figure out default choices for food that mutually optimize for time, nutrition, taste, and money.

In other words, if you were going to eat the same thing every day[^1], what would be the best choices for breakfast, lunch, dinner, & as a snack? 

I'm a large man[^2]. I am *(slightly)* more active than an average individual. Like anyone, I'd like to spend as few dollars as possible.
