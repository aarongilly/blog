# Languishing Doodle

**Excalidraw Doodle**

![[creation_languishing.png|250]]

## Details

I really like using [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com) to make little doodles (in addition to its whiteboarding features). 

I wanted to depict the feeling of being stuck doing something **useless and mindless** rather than the thing you *need* to do - or even the thing you *want* to do. It's like being "*on the fence*", but you can't simply "fall off" to either side. That would take too much energy. 

It's like being between two fences - **languishing in no-man's land**.
