A distilled list of principles I try to apply wherever I can.

1. **Use systems to manage your life**
	1. Have an inbox & clean it regularly
	2. Do periodic reviews
	3. Use checklists & templates
	4. Leverage standards and preexisting work
2. **Keep it simple**
	1. Do one thing instead of two
	2. Eliminate things before adding new things
	3. Prefer the option with the fewest moving pieces
3. **Bias toward action**
	1. Learn by doing
	2. Prefer quick, cheap empirical results over theory
	3. Prefer removing obstacles over adding effort
4. **Pay attention to what is important**
	1. Learn what's true
	2. Regularly reflect on life
	3. Remember that fun is important
	4. Use the Pareto Principle
	5. Measure what you want to manage
5. **Use maintenance as mindfulness**
	1. Don't skip workouts. Don't skip reps. Don't compromise form.
	2. Keep your bills paid and food on the table
	3. Take care of what's important
6. **Don't go it alone**
	1. Use your team
	2. Help others where you can

## Technology Principles

1. **Choose boring technology**
	1. Remember innovation tokens are limited
2. **Avoid proprietary file formats**
	1. Except niche capabilities (e.g. CAD Modeling, video editing)
	2. Use durable file types - the closer to plain text the better
3. **Choose local-first**
	1. Use the cloud, but avoid cloud-only solutions
4. **Avoid having multiple applications for the same function**
5. **Use SemVer Versioning & ISO 8601**
